Method

A single optical tile is passed through Depth Anything V2-Small, a zero-shot monocular depth vision transformer. Its relative inverse-depth output is robustly stretched to an 8-bit heightmap and projected as a displacement map on a 256×256 mesh.

Single-view depth is scale-ambiguous. DepthWizard is fail-closed: heights stay on a 0–100 relative scale until you ground them with a known point (Za) and a scale S, which can itself be solved from a second known point. Slope classes switch from relative ranking to real degrees once grounded.
